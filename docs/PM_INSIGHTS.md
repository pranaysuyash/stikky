## Executive Summary

StickerCraft is positioned to become the **"Canva for Stickers"** in the Indian market, with a clear path to 1M users in 12 months and ₹1Cr ARR by Year 2.

**Key Differentiators:**
1. Only India-first sticker app with Hinglish + Festival focus
2. Multi-platform (4 platforms vs competitors' 1-2)
3. AI-powered with zero design skills required
4. Built-in viral growth engine (referrals)

**Critical Metrics to Watch:**
- **North Star Metric**: Monthly Active Sticker Creators
- **Activation**: % who create first sticker (Target: >70%)
- **Retention**: D30 retention (Target: >40%)
- **Viral Coefficient**: K-factor (Target: >1.2)
- **Monetization**: Free-to-Paid conversion (Target: >5%)

## 3. Growth Strategy & Acquisition Channels

### Phase 1: Organic + Word-of-Mouth (Month 0-3)

**Target:** 10,000 users

**Tactics:**
1. **Product Hunt Launch**
   - Aim for top 5 product of the day
   - Prepare demo videos, GIFs
   - Coordinate with maker community

2. **Reddit Seeding**
   - r/india: "Built a Hinglish sticker maker"
   - r/whatsapp: Share use cases
   - r/SideProject: Showcase tech
   - Strategy: Authentic, helpful, not salesy

3. **Instagram/Twitter Presence**
   - Daily sticker of the day
   - User-generated content repost
   - Behind-the-scenes AI magic
   - Hashtags: #HinglishStickers #MadeWithStickerCraft

4. **WhatsApp Group Seeding**
   - Target tech communities, startup groups
   - Share festival packs during Diwali/Holi
   - Encourage organic sharing

**Budget:** ₹50k (design, content creation)

---

### Phase 2: Paid Acquisition + Influencer (Month 4-6)

**Target:** 50,000 users

**Tactics:**
1. **Micro-Influencer Partnerships**
   - 10-20 influencers (10k-100k followers)

2. **Instagram/Facebook Ads**
   - Targeting: Age 18-35, India, mobile users
   - Creative: Before/After (text → sticker)
   - Budget: ₹2L/month
   - Expected CPI: ₹20-30

3. **Festival Campaigns**
   - Diwali Pack Giveaway
   - Holi Color Sticker Challenge
   - Contest: Best sticker wins Pro for 1 year

4. **App Store Optimization (ASO)**
   - Keywords: sticker maker, WhatsApp stickers, AI stickers
   - Screenshots: Show stickers in WhatsApp
   - Reviews: Encourage happy users to rate

**Budget:** ₹5L

---

### Phase 3: Viral Growth + Scale (Month 7-12)

**Target:** 250,000+ users

**Tactics:**
1. **Referral Program Optimization**

   - Add leaderboard gamification

2. **Content Marketing**
   - Blog: "How to create Diwali stickers"
   - YouTube tutorials
   - TikTok/Reels: Quick tips

3. **Partnership**
   - WhatsApp Business API integration
   - Telegram Bot Store listing
   - Collaboration with meme pages

4. **PR & Media**
   - Tech publications (YourStory, Inc42)
   - Mainstream media during festivals
   - Awards: Apply for app awards

---

### Pricing Experiments to Run

#### Experiment 1: Tiered Onboarding
**Hypothesis:** Showing tier comparison at signup increases paid conversion

**Test:**
- Control: Standard signup flow
- Variant A: Show tier comparison after first sticker
- Variant B: Limited free trial of Creator (7 days)

**Metric:** 7-day paid conversion rate

---

#### Experiment 2: Credit Pricing
**Hypothesis:** Offering one-time credit packs increases revenue

**New Offering:**
- 50 credits: ₹99 (one-time)
- 200 credits: ₹349
- 500 credits: ₹799

**Expected:** 10% of users prefer one-time over subscription

---

#### Experiment 3: Annual Discount
**Hypothesis:** Annual plans increase LTV

**Pricing:**
- Creator Annual: ₹1,999 (₹390 savings = 16% off)
- Pro Annual: ₹4,999 (₹1,000 savings = 17% off)

**Target:** 30% of subscribers choose annual

## 7. A/B Testing Roadmap

### High-Priority Tests (Month 1-3)

#### Test 1: Onboarding Flow
**Hypothesis:** Interactive tutorial increases activation

**Variants:**
- A (Control): Static tutorial screens
- B: Interactive tutorial (create sticker during onboarding)
- C: Video tutorial

**Primary Metric:** % who create first sticker
**Sample Size:** 1,000 users per variant
**Runtime:** 7 days

---

#### Test 2: Paywall Timing
**Hypothesis:** Later paywall increases conversions

**Variants:**
- A (Control): Show paywall after 30 free exports
- B: Show after 50 exports
- C: Show after credit depletion

**Primary Metric:** Free-to-paid conversion rate
**Secondary:** User satisfaction score

---

#### Test 3: Referral Reward Amount
**Hypothesis:** Higher rewards increase referrals

**Variants:**
- A (Control): 20 credits each
- B: 30 credits each
- C: 20 for referee, 40 for referrer

**Primary Metric:** Referrals per user
**Watch:** Credit abuse patterns

---

### Medium-Priority Tests (Month 4-6)

#### Test 4: Template vs Blank Canvas Default
**Hypothesis:** Templates increase success rate

**Variants:**
- A: Show templates first
- B: Show blank canvas first
- C: Ask user preference

**Primary Metric:** Sticker creation success rate

---

#### Test 5: Social Proof
**Hypothesis:** Showing usage stats increases conversions

**Variants:**
- A: No social proof
- B: "10,000+ users created stickers today"
- C: User reviews on pricing page

**Primary Metric:** Subscription conversion

---

#### Test 6: AI Speed vs Quality
**Hypothesis:** Users prefer faster over perfect

**Variants:**
- A: 4-step generation (slower, better quality)
- B: 2-step generation (faster, slightly worse)

**Primary Metric:** User satisfaction + retention

## 10. Decision Framework

### When to Pivot?

**Red Flags (after 6 months):**
- Activation rate <50%
- D7 retention <20%
- Paid conversion <2%
- K-factor <0.5 (not viral)
- Churn rate >10%/month

**Green Flags (keep pushing):**
- Any metric above target
- Strong organic growth
- High NPS (>50)
- Media/investor interest
- Competitor validation (copying us)

---

### Feature Kill Criteria

**Kill a feature if:**
- <5% of users use it in a month
- <1% revenue attribution
- High maintenance cost vs value
- Negative user feedback
- Regulatory/legal risks

---

### Resource Allocation

**70-20-10 Rule:**
- 70% resources: Core product (sticker creation/export)
- 20% resources: Growth features (referrals, marketplace)
- 10% resources: Experiments (new AI models, web app)

## 11. North Star Metric Deep Dive

**Our North Star:** Monthly Active Sticker Creators (MASC)

**Why This Metric?**
- Leading indicator of engagement
- Correlates with retention
- Drives viral growth (more stickers = more sharing)
- Directly impacts revenue (active users convert)

**Target:** 60% of registered users = Monthly Active

**Calculation:**
```
MASC = Unique users who created ≥1 sticker in last 30 days
```

**Inputs (Sub-Metrics):**
1. New user signups
2. Activation rate
3. Retention rate
4. Resurrection rate (dormant → active)

**Levers to Increase MASC:**
- ✅ Improve onboarding (activation)
- ✅ Push notifications (re-engagement)
- ✅ Fresh content (festival packs, templates)
- ✅ Social features (challenges, sharing)
- ✅ Habit formation (streak rewards)
